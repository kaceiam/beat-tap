Pick a music file from your computer. It plays, and you click where you want each circle, right on the beat. Save it, and it stays here so you can play it any time. You can send the level to a friend too.

➕ Add your Spotify Liked Songs

1. In the Spotify app, go to Liked Songs.
2. Click on one song, then press Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C.
3. Click in the box below and press Ctrl+V.

Let the game hear your music 🎧

A box will pop up. In it:
1. Click Entire screen (or Screen) at the top.
2. Click your screen.
3. Turn ON Also share system audio at the bottom.
4. Click Share.

The game only listens to the sound, nothing else. You only do this once each time you open the game.

Paused ⏸

Put your mouse back where you want it, then press Resume.

How to play 🎵

🖱️

1. Circles fly at you from the middle of the screen, on the beat of the song.
2. Move your mouse inside a circle when it gets big. No clicking!
3. Right on the beat = PERFECT. Keep hitting them for a big combo.
4. Pink circles are words from the song.
5. Pick Easy to start. Insane is every beat. Press Esc to pause or stop.
